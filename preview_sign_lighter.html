Telegram avatar backgrounds

Custom emoji avatars occupy 67% of the circle. Color count splits the rest of the disc: 1 = solid, 2 = linear top→bottom (rotation 0), 3–4 = freeform blobs (① top-right, ② top-left, ③ bottom-left, ④ bottom-right). Enter colors in that order in Telegram.
